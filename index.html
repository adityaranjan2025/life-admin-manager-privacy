Privacy Policy for Life Admin Manager

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Life Admin Manager is a personal organization application designed to help users manage important documents, expiry dates, reminders, subscriptions, vehicle information, insurance, bills, warranties, appointments, and other personal administrative information.

1. Information Stored by the App

Life Admin Manager allows users to enter information such as:

  • Document names
  • Document types
  • Document numbers
  • Owner names
  • Issue dates
  • Expiry dates
  • Expiry times
  • Notes

This information is stored locally on the user's device.

2. Local Storage

Life Admin Manager is designed to operate without requiring an online account or cloud backend.

Information entered into the app is stored locally on the user's device using local storage/database technology. The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

Life Admin Manager does not intentionally collect or transmit the personal document information entered by users to the developer's servers or a cloud database.

The app does not sell, rent, or share users' document information with third parties.

4. Notifications

Life Admin Manager may use local notifications to remind users about upcoming expiry dates and other reminders.

Notifications are generated by the app on the user's device. Users can control notification and reminder settings within the app and through their device settings.

5. Document Security

Because information is stored locally on the user's device, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device, including device passwords, screen locks, and other security measures.

Users should not share their device with untrusted individuals if the device contains sensitive personal information.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete individual records from within the application.

Users may also remove the application from their device. Data stored locally by the application may be removed when the application data is deleted or the application is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of the user's device and operating system.

7. Third-Party Services

Life Admin Manager does not require third-party cloud services for storing the personal information entered into the application.

The application may use standard Android operating-system services, such as the Android notification system, to provide local notifications.

8. Children's Privacy

Life Admin Manager is intended for general users and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through online services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Life Admin Manager, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
